

# SOC Analyst Workflow


## Step 1

Alert Detection


The platform receives security events.



## Step 2

Analysis


Analyst reviews:

- Logs
- Indicators
- Risk Score
- Threat Intelligence



## Step 3

Investigation


Create case:

- Evidence Collection
- Timeline
- Analyst Notes



## Step 4

Response


Execute:

- Playbooks
- Automated Actions
- Escalation



## Step 5

Reporting


Generate:

- Security Reports
- Executive Reports
