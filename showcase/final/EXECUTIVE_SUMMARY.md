

# MUTEB SOC Enterprise


## Executive Summary


MUTEB SOC Enterprise is a defensive cybersecurity platform designed to demonstrate Security Operations Center capabilities and Blue Team workflows.


The platform simulates enterprise security operations including:


- Monitoring

- Detection

- Investigation

- Response

- Reporting



## Business Value


Provides:


- Security Visibility

- Faster Incident Analysis

- Threat Identification

- Operational Reporting



## Target Environment


Enterprise SOC Teams

Security Operations Departments

Cybersecurity Training Labs
